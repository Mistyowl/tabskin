Why a minimalist new tab improves focus

Heavy new tab dashboards add decisions before you even start working. A calm wallpaper and clock do the opposite.

The cost of a busy new tab

Feeds, to-do widgets, weather, and news on every new tab create micro-distractions. Each Ctrl+T becomes a small context switch.

What minimalism changes

A minimalist new tab — wallpaper, time, nothing else — gives visual pleasure without pulling attention into another task list or headline.
