How to change the new tab in Chrome

Chrome's default new tab is often empty or cluttered with shortcuts. Tabskin replaces it with a calm wallpaper page.

Why change the new tab?

You open new tabs dozens of times a day. A beautiful background and clock can start your session more calmly than a blank screen.

Method: Tabskin extension

The easiest way is to install Tabskin for Chrome. It overrides the new tab page without system-level changes.

What you get

  • Unsplash wallpapers by theme
  • Configurable clock
  • Local settings, no ads
