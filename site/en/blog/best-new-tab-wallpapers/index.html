Best new tab wallpapers

Quality photos make every new tab feel better. Tabskin uses curated Unsplash collections.

Why Unsplash?

Unsplash offers professional photos with an open license. Tabskin loads images via API and shows authors — important for platform compliance.

Wallpaper themes in Tabskin

In settings you can pick a category: nature, architecture, travel, textures, and more. The theme sets the mood of every new tab background.

Tips for choosing

  • Calm landscapes and architecture work well for focus
  • Bold textures suit creative tasks
  • Switch themes if the background starts to distract
