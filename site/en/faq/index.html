Frequently asked questions about Tabskin

Answers about the new tab extension: privacy, installation, Unsplash wallpapers, and supported browsers.

What is Tabskin?

A free Chrome and Firefox extension that replaces the default new tab with a minimalist page featuring Unsplash wallpapers and a clock.

Does Tabskin collect personal data?

No. Settings stay local. See our privacy policy for details.

Are there ads?

No. The new tab stays clean without ads or cluttered widgets.

Which browsers are supported?

Google Chrome and Mozilla Firefox.

Where do wallpapers come from?

Photos are loaded via Unsplash API with proper author attribution.

Follow our guides: Chrome install or Firefox install.
